Water is the cost that decides everything.

In the Souss-Massa basin, most blocks are still irrigated by routine. The pump runs when it has always run. Water and diesel cost more every year, and much of the valley draws on groundwater that is not being replaced, so that routine gets more expensive every season.

What is usually missing is a weekly answer to a plain question: how much water, in which block, this week.

The method

How the weekly number is made

01

Satellite passes

Sentinel-2 and Landsat pass over the valley every few days. The images show how each block is coping between irrigations.

02

The FAO-56 calculation

The FAO-56 method turns weather and crop stress into millimetres of water per block. We calibrate the calculation with soil measurements taken on the farm.

03

A weekly card

The amounts arrive once a week, block by block, on WhatsApp or on paper. You read the card and run the pumps accordingly.

Pricing

You pay from the savings.

The fee is a share of what you save: 20 percent of the water cost the farm measurably cuts. There is no fixed fee and no equipment to buy. If the water bill does not go down, you pay nothing.
